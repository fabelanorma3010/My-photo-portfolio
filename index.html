Why Chicago?

Chicago has the best atractions like the wills tower

Chicago is the birthplace of the skyscraper and features over 1,250 high-rises, anchored by the iconic Willis Tower at 1,451 feet. Willis Tower—originally and still commonly known as the Sears Tower—is a 110-story skyscraper in Chicago, Illinois, designed by architect Bruce Graham and structural engineer Fazlur Rahman Khan of Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. You can visit the 103rd floor observation deck, step out onto glass floored balconiies,explore interactive lower-level exhibits, and enjoy multi-level dinning and public rooftop terrance.
